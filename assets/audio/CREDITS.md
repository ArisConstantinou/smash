# IRONFALL — recorded audio credits

These recordings are bundled with the game. No third-party service is contacted during playback.

## Physical impacts (12 clips)
**Impact Sounds** by **Kenney (Kenney.nl)**.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original license is included as `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
Selected punch, metal, mining and wood recordings were trimmed, peak-normalized,
faded and converted to mono MP3. No endorsement by Kenney is implied.

## Player effort (3 clips)
**Generic Hero Effort Noises / Valiant Genericus (Effort Noises)** by **tinsin**.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/generic-hero-effort-noises
License selected: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Short excerpts at 0.235–0.480 s, 0.750–1.100 s and 1.500–1.980 s were extracted,
trimmed, peak-normalized, faded and converted to mono MP3. Runtime pitch may vary.
These are recordings of a human performer, not speech synthesis. No endorsement
by the performer is implied. Attribution must accompany redistribution.

## Pain / enemy reactions (4 clips)
**Hurt Sound Effects** by **EZduzziteh**.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/hurt-sound-effects
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source files `hurt_01.mp3` through `hurt_04.mp3` were trimmed, normalized, faded
and converted to mono MP3. Playback rate varies with enemy type.

The source hashes, output hashes and excerpt mapping are in `manifest.json`.
`python3 tools/build-audio.py` reproduces the recorded bank using FFmpeg and
verified source downloads. Existing procedural swing air, debris, ambience,
robot reactions and reward stingers are original game effects, not recordings.

## Progressive destruction and vehicle alarms (damage8)
Car-panel tears, falling rubble and building-collapse events layer the existing
CC0 Kenney metal/mining recordings with original procedural glass shards,
structural rumble and settling debris. Three original synthesized automotive
alarm patterns are selected per car and attenuated by distance. No additional
recordings or third-party audio requests were introduced. These electronic
sirens are synthesized effects, not recordings of a particular vehicle.

## Sonic Scream / rage
**Male Yelling out a War Cry 1 - WITHOUT reverb.wav** performed by
**WelvynZPorterSamples**. Source: https://freesound.org/s/621342/
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The preview recording was excerpted, time-compressed without shifting pitch,
filtered, normalized and encoded to mono MP3. The pressure layers are original
procedural effects mixed in the game.
